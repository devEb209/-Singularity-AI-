# NMN — Natural Mindset of NPCs

Complement of the D Thesis and Real-Life Universal. NMN turns characters into contextual digital agents. It does **not** claim consciousness, feelings or subjective experience.

## Operational scope

- Identity, personality, individual knowledge, relationships, values, goals and needs.
- Non-omniscient perception: unknown / rumor / incomplete / witnessed.
- Hierarchical memory: active, recent, important, consolidated, historical.
- Contextual utility decisions. The same invasion does not force every civilian to flee.
- Social observation and optional crowd-following. Emergence is detected, not scheduled.
- D-O15 fidelity: dormant → low → medium → high → full, preserving identity.
- Persistent file-manager layout under `NPC/{id}/...`.
- Voice contract is `adapter-required`. No fake speech is generated.

## War fixture

`POST /api/v1/nmn/scenarios/war` builds seven distinct characters and verifies that doctor, soldier, denier, terrified civilian and family-driven people diverge without a global script.

## Remaining

Navigation meshes, large populations, persistent autonomous lives at city scale and connected voice models remain partial.
