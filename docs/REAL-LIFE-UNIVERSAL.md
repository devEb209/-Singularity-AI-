# Real-Life Universal

Complement of the D Thesis. Not a closed “realism pack” and not a photorealism promise.

## Operational scope

- Extensible domain catalog (natural sciences, behavior, engineering, natural and artificial systems). New domains can be registered; the list is not closed.
- Reality modes: `real-life`, `cartoon`, `toon-force`, `fantasy`, `sci-fi`, `stylized`, `surreal`, `custom`.
- Knowledge pipeline: observe → research → comprehend → abstract → model → simulate → integrate → validate → optimize.
- Law consistency: conservation-breaking energy gain is rejected when conservation is required.
- Environment graph with damped coupling (climate ↔ water ↔ soil ↔ vegetation ↔ fauna ↔ human ↔ lighting ↔ materials ↔ atmosphere).
- Graphics phenomena ranked by objective. The goal is to model causes of appearance, not to imitate a photograph.
- Perceptual equivalence and hardware redistribution through D-O15.
- D-Genesis emits a **spec** when no domain fits. It does not execute generated code.

## Honest limits

External GIS/maps/climate APIs are not fetched. Incoming sources are classified; unsourced text is speculation. This is not a complete scientific simulator and does not claim a physical fourth dimension.
